How it works

Three parts, working as one system.

Brio3 isn't a voice bot with data bolted on. Voice, ICP, and data are engineered to feed each other — here's how they combine, and what happens once you're on board.

The targeting layer

ICP

Everything starts with who's actually worth reaching. Our team defines and tests your ideal customer profile — the segments, titles, firmographics, and signals that describe a real buyer for you.

Built by operators who've defined ICPs before, not guessed from a form.
Tested and refined against real call outcomes, so it sharpens over time.
Becomes the filter that everything downstream runs through.
The sourcing layer

Data

Once the ICP is set, we find the real people who match it. Brio3 aggregates and reconciles contact and account data across multiple providers rather than trusting a single source.

Pulls from providers like [ZoomInfo, Cognism, Apollo] and reconciles conflicts between them.
De-duplicates and verifies so the agent isn't dialing dead numbers.
Produces a clean, current call list mapped to the ICP.
The execution layer

Voice

Then the voice agent actually works the list — placing outbound calls and handling inbound ones as a real caller would, at a volume and consistency a human team can't sustain.

Phone-native: holds a live conversation, not a scripted IVR tree.
Runs both directions — outbound campaigns and inbound response.
Call outcomes flow back to sharpen the ICP, closing the loop.
